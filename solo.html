Georgia born with a Mississippi twist. A little edge on the country, peppered with blues, R&B and a big helping of soul.

Raised on the red clay

Spencer has been performing since he was nine, with ensembles around the world, and picked up a music degree along the way. The originals drip with smooth melodies and imagery that make you ask whether you have heard that song before.

The set wraps them in an eclectic run of the best songs you forgot you loved, across decades and genres: John Mellencamp, Marcy Playground, Otis Redding, Sam Cooke, Barenaked Ladies, Eric Clapton, David Allan Coe, The Black Crowes. His single My Saving Grace placed on the Georgia Songbirds Weekly Top 10 confirm placement.

Solo and duo shows

Two to four hours, acoustic. Breweries, patios, restaurants, listening rooms. Often with Mark Grundhoefer on second guitar.

Private events

Weddings, company parties, backyard shows. Originals on request, covers by the decade.

Hosting and mentoring

Weekly trivia and open mic at MadLife Stage & Studios. Coach for the Rhythm Ranch Youth Singer-Songwriter Collective.
