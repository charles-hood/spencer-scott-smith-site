The Cherokee Roses

Named for the wild rose that grows along Georgia's backroads, the Cherokee Roses are the full-band version of what Spencer has been doing solo for twenty-five years: country with an edge, peppered with blues, R&B and a big helping of soul.

Spencer was raised on the red clay river banks of North Georgia and has been performing since he was nine. He plays around two hundred nights a year, most of them solo on patios and in listening rooms across the north metro. The Roses are for the nights that call for drums.

They made their full-band debut at MadLife Stage & Studios in Woodstock in February 2026, recorded live for a TV episode and a live album, then played 529 in East Atlanta over the Fourth of July weekend and the You42 festival in Roswell in September. The first studio single, Spinning 'Round Again, is out November 11.
