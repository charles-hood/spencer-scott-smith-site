The Story

Raised on the red clay
river banks of North Georgia.

If you like a little edge on your country, peppered with blues, R&B, and a big helping of soul, then search no more. Spencer's original music drips with smooth melodies and imagery that will make you curious — "have I heard that song before?"

The catchy originals are wrapped in an eclectic mix of some of the best songs you forgot you loved — spanning decades and genres, from John Mellencamp and Otis Redding to Sam Cooke, Eric Clapton, and The Black Crowes. Performing since he was nine, with ensembles around the world for over 25 years and a degree in music to show for it, he's now stepping his songwriting into the spotlight and making a real mark on the North Georgia scene.
